# Pindi Boy v0.2.0: the trick update

Wheelies were doing all the work. Now there's a real stunt system.

**▶ Play: https://ahab94.github.io/pindi-boy/**

- **JUMP (bunny hop):** hold to crouch and charge, let go to pop into the air, anywhere on the road. Hold it as you hit a ramp (PRELOAD) for huge air.
- **Flips:** in the air, hold BACK FLIP (Up) or FRONT FLIP (Down). The bike turns only while you hold, so let go to stop it. Land level or you land on your head. Doubles and triples pay big.
- **360 spins:** in the air, SPIN (on keyboard Left/Right) whips the bike round. A half spin lands sloppy.
- **Grabs you hold:** hold NO HANDS, SUPERMAN, SEAT STAND or SELFIE to keep the pose going. TWEAKED = more views. Let go before the wheels touch.
- **JIGRA meter:** every stunt fills it. Full = **SCENE ON!**: ten seconds of double views, a speed boost with silencer flames, and the trick buttons become super tricks: BHANGRA ON THE SEAT, HANDSTAND, ULTA VLOG.
- **Wheelies link it all:** stay on one wheel between jumps to keep a combo alive.
- **Smoother:** Shani's poses blend into each other, the bike squashes on landing and leans in lane changes.
- **The coach and How to play** cover the new moves. On phones the buttons relabel in the air (BACK FLIP / FRONT FLIP / SPIN), and JUMP sits above WHEELIE.
- New moves arrive ride by ride: JUMP on ride 2, flips and JIGRA on ride 3, spins on ride 5. Targets are retuned for the new scoring.
