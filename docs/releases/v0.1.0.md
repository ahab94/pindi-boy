# Pindi Boy v0.1.0

The first release. Shani has borrowed Abbu's 70, and every dumb stunt is content.

**▶ Play in your browser (phone or computer): https://ahab94.github.io/pindi-boy/**

- 10 rides around Pindi: gali wheelies, speed breakers, Raja Bazaar traffic, the pataakha silencer, plank ramps, the warden, the 125, rain, a baraat and Ammi's chappals.
- Stunts: wheelies, NO HANDS, SUPERMAN, SEAT STAND and SELFIE, plus close calls, pigeon chaos and jumping over thelas and buses.
- Combos multiply your views. Three crashes and Abbu takes the keys.
- Touch controls on phones and tablets, in portrait or landscape. The bike speeds up by itself on touch screens.
- Pothohari music and every sound synthesized, plus a live chat full of Pindi slang.

**Downloads:** Windows, macOS (universal) and Linux zips are below. The one-line installers are in the README.
