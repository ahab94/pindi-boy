# Pindi Boy v0.2.2: one TRICK button

Fewer buttons on phones. The four grab buttons and PATAAKHA are now one **TRICK** button with a quick selector:

- **Hold TRICK** and the tricks fan out above your thumb. **Slide to one** and it starts immediately, and keeps going while you hold (so grabs still tweak). Let go to finish.
- **A quick tap** repeats your last trick.
- During **SCENE ON** the selector shows the super tricks.
- The coach points at TRICK (and lights up the trick it means in the selector).

That leaves six buttons: UP, DOWN and BRAKE on the left (BACK FLIP, FRONT FLIP and SPIN in the air), and WHEELIE, JUMP and TRICK on the right. Keyboard controls are unchanged.
