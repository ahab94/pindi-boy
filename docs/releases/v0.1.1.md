# Pindi Boy v0.1.1

Thanks for the first round of feedback. This update fixes sound in the browser and makes stunts learnable rather than luck.

**▶ Play: https://ahab94.github.io/pindi-boy/**

- **Sound in the browser:** music and effects now play in the web version. (Godot's default web audio mode came out silent for this game, so it now uses the same mixer as desktop.) On iPhone it plays even with the silent switch on.
- **Easier wheelies:** hold WHEELIE and the bike balances itself. The wheelie gauge now sits right behind the bike, so you can watch the road at the same time. The needle slowly creeps toward red, and "LET GO!" flashes before you loop it.
- **Forgiving tricks:** landing before a trick finishes is now a SLOPPY landing (you lose the combo) instead of a crash. A trick that can't fit in the air time left is refused with "TOO LOW!".
- **A coach:** hints such as "Speed breaker ahead: jump, then tap NO HANDS" and "NOW! Tap Superman", with the right button glowing on touch screens. There are full hints on the first four rides, and later only for tricks you haven't landed yet.
- **How to play:** a new page on the menu explains views, wheelies, combos and tricks in one screen.
- A ring around Shani fills while a trick is being done.
- Targets retuned for the easier controls.
